# Non-Visual Melee changes

What changed in each release of Non-Visual Melee, newest first.

## Unreleased

### New

- **Positional sound effects:** each fighter's own sounds, such as attacks,
  jumps, landings and hits, now come from where that fighter is and grow
  quieter with distance, instead of always playing in the middle. Your own
  fighter's sounds stay in the middle. Switch it off, or set it, in Cue
  settings.
- **Stage sounds switch:** mutes the sounds a stage makes of its own, such
  as Corneria's Arwings or Mute City's cars, so the fighters are easier to
  hear. In Cue settings. The stage's echo is the separate Reverb setting.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-24)

Plays online with Melee-PC v0.4.2.

### New

The first beta. What it reads and plays:

- **Modes:** Training, Multi-Man Melee, and VS. Melee and Special Melee, on one
  computer or online.
- **The launcher and the port menu**, read aloud, with an Accessibility
  category in both for the switches, volumes, voices and a preview of each
  sound. The launcher tells you when a newer Non-Visual Melee release is out.
- **The game's menus:** the main menu and its screens, the rules screens,
  Options, Training's pause menu, the results screen, memory card prompts and
  unlock notices.
- **Character select and stage select:** the D-pad steps the cursor from one
  thing to the next, saying where it goes.
- **Matches:** two voices announce damage, stocks and the items players take,
  and audio cues tell you where you and the nearest opponent are, warn of gaps
  and ledges, and lead you to items.
- **Online play** over LAN and Direct Connect, against Non-Visual Melee and
  melee-pc players, with melee-netcheck to test your network.

The [Non-Visual Melee guide](https://github.com/ohylli/non-visual-melee/blob/master/ACCESSIBILITY.md)
says what is and is not accessible yet, and how to get started.
