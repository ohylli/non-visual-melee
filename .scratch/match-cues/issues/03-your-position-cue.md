# 03 The match tick hook and your position cue

Status: resolved (2026-10-13)
Type: task
Blocked by: 01, 02

The first cue in a match, with the hook and the snapshot every later cue reads. Read the spec's "When cues sound", "Who is you, and who is the opponent", "The stage frame", "Your position cue" and "How the fork attaches" first, and `docs/a11y/gameplay-state.md` ("Reading safely", "When to read: after the fighters move", "Position and motion", "Stage identity, blast zones, camera and points").

## Machinery

- **The tick hook**: `pc_a11y_match_tick(uint64_t held_mask)`, one line beside `pc_slp_tick_end` in `gm_RunSimTick` (`src/melee/gm/gmscene.c`), inside the base port's existing `TARGET_PC` block, declared in `a11y_hooks.h`. Silent while `pc_net_resim()` is true. It also calls the cue settings' `poll()`.
- **Match or not**: the hook runs in every scene that uses `gm_RunSimTick`, menus included, so the snapshot says whether a match with fighters is running; outside one, nothing sounds.
- **Pause and GAME!**: the fighters' bit in `held_mask` for more than a few ticks fades every voice out; the fighters moving again fades them back in. Look for a direct pause flag that tells pause from a slow-motion mode's skipped ticks (spec, open questions), and use it if one turns up; record the finding in the primer either way.
- **The match snapshot**, filled in C in `game_access.c` (compiled with the game's bitfield layout): you and the opponent, each with position, ground or air, knocked out or between death and respawn (`motion_id` 0 to 11), and team; the stage frame (the floors' extent, side and bottom blast zones, camera box top). You are port 1 offline and `pc_net_local_player()` online, the slot's main fighter; the opponent is the nearest other main fighter in play, not on your team, kept as a list of one.
- **The driver**, a pure C++ function from the snapshot and the cue's settings to your voice's targets:
  - Pan piecewise linear in x: main stage centre 0, its edges ±edge pan, side blast zones ±1.
  - Pitch piecewise linear in octaves of y: main floor 0, camera box top + up range, bottom blast zone − down range.
  - Interval on a log scale from slow at the far distance and beyond to fast at the near distance; slow with no opponent.
  - Silent while you are knocked out or between death and respawn.
- **The sound**: a generated tick, about 12 ms of lead-in silence then a damped sine of about 20 ms (the Star Fox 64 fork's aim click, `AimCue_GenerateClick`). It must fit the fast interval's floor.
- **Settings** registered: on or off, volume, boost (issue 02's helper), and edge pan 0.7, up range 1 octave, down range 1 octave, slow interval 0.8 s, fast interval 0.08 s, far distance 120 units, near distance 10 units, each with a sensible range.

## Done when

- A drive script under `tools/a11y/` starts a Training match on Final Destination, walks to each edge, jumps off and comes back, then pauses and unpauses. Its log shows your cue starting at the match, stopping and starting with the pause, and stopping at the scene change; the trace's pan and pitch follow the walk; the analysis script on the dump shows the balance swinging and the pulse speeding up as the fighter nears the CPU.
- `python tools/check_style.py` passes; the footprint grep shows the tick hook.
- The `verify` skill lists the script and its expected lines.
- `docs/a11y/gameplay-state.md` records the tick hook as built and the pause finding; `docs/a11y/cues.md` describes the match snapshot and the drivers.
- `CONTEXT.md` gains "Position cue"; CLAUDE.md's accessibility status gains the line for match cues.

## Comments

### 2026-10-13, implementation (agent)

Implemented; your position cue ticks in every match.

- **Where things live.** `pc_a11y_match_tick` in `gm_RunSimTick` beside `pc_slp_tick_end`: two lines in `gmscene.c`, both inside the base port's `TARGET_PC` blocks (the call, and the header's include beside the base port's own). `game_access.c`: the match snapshot (`A11yMatchState`, `a11y_game_match_state`). `position_cue.hpp/.cpp`: the pure driver (`stage_pan`, `stage_pitch`, `nearness_interval`, `your_position`). `match_cues.hpp/.cpp`: `MatchCues`, which registers the cue's settings and voice in `open_cue_settings` and turns each tick's snapshot into the voice's start, targets or stop. `hooks.cpp`: the hook, gated on `pc_net_resim()`, polling the settings in a match.
- **Pause versus slow motion: a direct flag exists, and the held mask alone would have been wrong.** The game's pause word (`gm_80479D58.unk_10.x0`, read with `gm_801A4624()`) has a bit per reason: 1 a VS pause, 2 a Training pause, 3 the pause camera, 4 GAME!, 5 a score tally, 6 an event's freeze, 0 the debug pause. A Training pause holds no GObj list at all (its bit maps to an empty mask; the fighters' own update stands still on it), so the held-mask grace would have missed it. Slow motion skips ticks through the frame timer with the word clear. Measured: a VS pause set 0x2 with the fighters held, GAME! 0x10 with them held, Training's menu 0x4 with nothing held, and Training at half speed held every other tick with the word clear. The snapshot's `paused` is the word being nonzero, and no grace is needed. Recorded in `gameplay-state.md`, "Pause, freezes and slow motion".
- **The pause in the log.** Pausing stops the voice and unpausing starts it again, rather than turning the mix level off: a stop is the engine's 5 ms ramp, which a 20 ms click cannot tell from a fade, the log shows each pause as this issue asks, and the pulse that comes at once on unpausing marks the match going on.
- **Before GO** the cue is silent: the fighters tick from the first frame, but their input stays off until GO (`input_disabled`), so the cue starts at GO. Training has no GO and starts at once.
- **The scene change** came with the cue already stopped in every exit met (Training's Finish is in its pause menu; a VS match ends on GAME!), so `stop_all` at scene entry had nothing to stop and logged nothing. It stays as the safety net.
- **Who is you.** As the spec says, plus one fallback: offline with no human slot on controller 1 (a single-player mode started from controller 2), the first human slot, which is the player character select followed. Online strictly `pc_net_local_player()`'s slot. Each `css_net.drive` instance's cue followed its own player (pan -0.79 on a, +0.79 on b).
- **The height reference and stage frame on the tournament stages.** The floors' extent is exact on Battlefield, Final Destination, Dream Land and Pokémon Stadium (neutral), off by 1.3 units on Fountain of Dreams while a side platform sinks, and wrong on Yoshi's Story: Randall the cloud is an enabled platform line, dragging the extent's bottom to -33.2 or -13.6 and its side out to about -103. So the snapshot finds the main floor itself, as the spec's height reference describes it: the lowest enabled floor line across the middle of the blast zones, a solid floor before a platform (Randall passes under the middle), followed to its surface's ends with `mpFloorGetLeft` and `mpFloorGetRight`; the extent only where no floor crosses the middle. Measured main floors, all at y = 0: Final Destination ±85.6, Battlefield ±68.4, Dream Land ±77.3, Fountain of Dreams ±63.3, Pokémon Stadium ±87.8, Yoshi's Story ±56 (its slopes end at y = -3.5). The table is in `gameplay-state.md`, "The tournament stages' frames". Pokémon Stadium's other forms were not reached.
- **Mapping details.** Pitch is held at the camera box's top and at the bottom blast zone rather than extrapolated past them; pan is held at ±1 past the side blast zones. The interval interpolates the log of the interval linearly in distance (the Star Fox 64 fork's obstacle cue), so each unit of distance closed speeds the tick by the same ratio. A near distance at or past the far one gives fast within it and slow beyond.
- **The sound and its limits.** The Star Fox 64 fork's aim click: 12 ms of silence, then a 1500 Hz sine decaying at 150 per second over 20 ms with a 3 ms faded tail. Its 32 ms last 64 ms an octave down, so the fast interval goes down to 0.07 s and the down range up to 1 octave, and the voice is made for exactly those (`MatchCues::kFastestInterval`, `kWidestDownRange`). Boost starts at 1.5. The other ranges: edge pan 0 to 1, up range 0 to 2 octaves (the engine's highest pitch is 4), slow interval 0.1 to 3 s, far distance 10 to 400 units, near distance 0 to 100 units. The up and down ranges carry no unit in the file, with "octaves" in their descriptions, since "default 1 octaves" read badly.
- **Settings wording, beyond the issue.** Issue 02's per-cue descriptions read "Turns the your position cue on or off"; `in_sentence` now leaves "the" out before a title starting with "Your".
- **Settings error at match start.** On a match's first tick the hook queues `SettingsFile::problem()` again when the last read had one, and it polls the file through the match.
- **Odd stages, noted.** On Princess Peach's Castle both players started below the floor found across the middle (pitch 0.87 online): its middle is the raised castle roof. The spec leaves odd stages for later.
- **Base port crash on Venom, not chased.** Venom crashes every time as its match starts, cues on or off, on the base port build of the last merge too: a read of 0xFFFFFFFFFFFFFFFF in `lb_8000B1CC` called from `grVenom_8020454C`, probably issue 01's "separate base port crash". `css_training.drive` and `css_classic.drive` fail when their random stage is Venom; the `verify` skill has it as a known failure. One Dream Land probe run also crashed once in aurora's pipeline worker thread (`aurora::gfx::create_timed`), a graphics flake that did not recur.
- **Verification**, online level, with `MELEE_A11Y_CUES=1` exported for every scripted run: build with the "Rollback sections" line; style; unit tests (`position_cue` new, covering everything the issue lists plus paused, before GO and slow motion; `launcher_data` fails as known); a bounded `title` run with cues on; `match_position.drive` three times in a row on its final form, each with six cue lines, the trace following the walk (x -60 to -80.8, off the edge to -96 at y -6 and back, a run to about +30, over the CPU to about +70) and the dump's pan swinging from -0.68 to +0.60 and its interval falling from 0.800 to 0.08; the sixteen menu drive scripts (`css_classic.drive` and `css_training.drive` each failed once on Venom and passed on rerun; every other script reaching a match logged its cue's start); the five `css_net.drive` runs and `lan_lobby.drive`, with no desync and the recordings agreeing. The maintainer's settings folder held no `a11y-cues.ini` afterwards.
- **For issue 05.** The snapshot already keeps the opponent (a list of one) with position, ground or air, out and team, and `stage_pan` and `stage_pitch` take any position. So the opponent's driver is a sibling of `your_position` with its own settings group from `add_cue`, its own mapping handles and a fixed interval, and a second voice in `MatchCues` whose sound fits its own interval at its own lowest pitch.
