# 03 Preview: a short sample of each cue

Status: resolved (2026-10-20)
Type: task
Blocked by: 01

`.scratch/accessibility-settings/spec.md`, "Preview". A way for fork code to play about two seconds of a cue at a fixed, typical moment in a match, outside any match.

## Machinery

- **The call.** `CueSystem` gains a preview of a cue by its id (the id `add_cue` takes) and a stop. Each cue supplies its own fixed targets beside its driver: the position cues a few slow pulses from the centre at middle height, the gap warning its hum ramped in and out, the ledge warning a few pulses on the left then on the right. Exact targets are set by ear; the tunable values live in code only.
- **Levels.** A preview ignores the cue's switch, the Cues switch and the Accessibility switch, and honours the master volume and mute, Cue volume and the cue's own volume and boost.
- **Restart and stop.** A second preview restarts it; a stop ends it with the usual ramp.
- **The port menu.** Its per-frame silencing (`silence_for_port_menu`) and `scene_entered` stop only the match's voices, so a preview plays on in the port menu and in the launcher.
- **Online.** The preview plays on the cue engine only, never through the game's sounds.

## Done when

- Unit tests on the engine's mix: a preview sounds with every switch off, is silent at a volume of 0, restarts, stops with a ramp, and survives the port menu's silencing, while the match's voices are still silenced.
- A hand run through the cue dump (`MELEE_A11Y_CUE_DUMP`, `tools/a11y/cue_dump.py`) shows each preview's sound, triggered through whatever test seam the implementer adds, removed afterwards or kept as a debug switch named in the `verify` skill.
- The `verify` skill's quick level passes, and `python tools/check_style.py`.
- `docs/a11y/cues.md` describes Preview and why the port menu silences only the match.

## Comments

### 2026-10-20, implementation (agent)

Implemented; each cue has a preview, played on voices of its own that ignore the switches, and the port menu's silence and scene entry stop only the match's voices.

- **Where things live.** Each cue's moment is a pure function beside its driver, from the seconds since the preview began to its voices' targets, none once it has ended: `your_position_preview` and `opponent_position_preview` (`position_cue.hpp`), `gap_warning_preview` (`gap_warning.hpp`), `ledge_warning_preview` (`ledge_warning.hpp`); the shared length and pulse count are in the new header-only `cue_preview.hpp`. `MatchCues` plays them: `preview(id, now)`, `stop_preview(now)`, `previewing()` and `preview_frame(now)`, with a preview twin of every match voice (`preview_of`: the same sound, named `<voice> preview`, made with `ignores_switches`). `CueSystem` forwards with the same names and logs `cue preview: <id>` (or `no cue <id>`) before the voices' start lines.
- **The moments**, at the cue's current values: the position cues centred at their own pitch, at the slow interval (yours) or the steady one (the opponent's), the pulse count nearest two seconds and at least two; the hum rising over a short ramp by its gain target, held and falling away by two seconds; the ledge warning's chord, a few pulses on the left, a pulse's pause, as many on the right, at the pan and level `ledge_side` gives halfway into `ledge_warning.distance`. Values in the code only.
- **Levels.** A new `VoiceSpec::ignores_switches` makes the engine's chain take the mix's and the cue's volume and boost and leave out both levels' `on`, so Accessibility (through `CueSettings::set_allowed`), Cues and the cue's own switch are all passed, and master volume and mute, Cue volume and the cue's volume and boost still hold.
- **Restart.** The engine carries a voice stopped and started within its ramp on where it was, so a stop and start in the same frame would not restart a pulse train. A preview's voices, once stopped, wait `MatchCues::kPreviewRestart` before starting again (`m_preview_quiet`), past the ramp and an audio block, so a second press starts from silence with a pulse at once, and is heard as a restart. Another cue's preview stops the one playing and waits the same way.
- **Who moves it.** `preview_frame` runs from `pc_a11y_launcher_frame` and from `pc_a11y_port_menu_frame`, which runs at every frame boundary with the menu open or not, before the hook's speech gate (a preview is no speech and never re-run by rollback). The game thread's frame times end it, so a stop lands within a frame of its time; the pulses themselves are on the audio clock.
- **The port menu.** `MatchCues::silence` stops the match's five voices by name instead of `stop_all`, and `CueSystem::scene_entered` now calls it too (it also forgets the gap warning's hold there, harmless at a scene change). `stop_all` stays in the engine, unused by the game.
- **Test seam, kept as a debug switch.** `MELEE_A11Y_CUE_PREVIEW=<id>,<id>...` (`Config::cue_preview`) plays those previews in turn from the run's first frame, a pause apart, named in the `verify` skill's Cues bullet and in `docs/a11y/cues.md`. It is also a way for the maintainer to hear the previews before issue 04's rows exist.
- **Tests.** `cue_engine`: a voice past the switches keeps every volume, and master, mix and cue volume at 0 still silence it. `position_cue`: both position previews centred, their intervals, pulse counts and ends, and two pulses at a slow interval past two seconds. `match_cues`, rendered at 10 ms frames: every preview sounds with every switch off (onsets at the expected times, the hum's rise and fall, the ledge left then right), silent at cue volume, the cue's volume and master volume 0 while still playing, a second press restarting after the wait, another cue's preview stopping the first, a stop ramping out with no step past the hum's own and nothing after, and a ledge preview playing to its end through a silence every frame while the match's gap hum is stopped.
- **Verification.** Build with the "Rollback sections" line; style; unit tests, all passing but `launcher_data` (known). A bounded `title` run. `run_level.py` with `MELEE_A11Y_CUES=1`: the seven match scripts, the menus and the online level, 31 of 31 (the quick level, plus menus for `hooks.cpp` and online for the cues). By hand, `drive.py --launcher` with `MELEE_A11Y_CUES=0` (so Accessibility and Cues both off) and all four ids plus an unknown one, through the cue dump: `cue_dump.py --window 0.5` showed your tick at 1500 Hz centred with three onsets 0.800 s apart, the opponent's pulse at 400 Hz with five 0.400 s apart, the hum at 120 Hz rising and falling, the chord with four onsets at pan -0.57 then four at +0.57, and `cue preview: no cue nonsense`. And a debug VS match (`--muted`, `MELEE_A11Y_CUES=1`) with the gap preview repeated: F1 stopped the match's three sounding voices, and a gap preview started and ran its full length while the menu stayed open, the match's voices starting again only after "Back to game"; the first preview had also played on across the boot's scene changes.
- **Docs.** `docs/a11y/cues.md`: a "Preview" section (the moments, the voices of its own, who moves it, why the port menu silences only the match, online), the volume chain's `ignores_switches`, the lifecycle bullets on scene entry and the port menu, and the debug switch under "Checking a cue without hearing it". The `verify` skill: the debug switch with its log lines and what the dump shows, and the `match_cues` suite's preview cases.
- **For issue 04.** Calls, all on the game thread, outside the simulation (the launcher's loop and the port menu run there): `CueSystem::preview(action->group, Clock::now())` on Enter or A on a Preview row, which returns false and logs for an unknown id; `CueSystem::stop_preview(Clock::now())` when the row loses focus, the page or menu closes, or the category is left; `CueSystem::previewing()` if the row wants to know. Nothing else is needed per frame: the two frame hooks already move it along. The instance is `s_cue_system` in `hooks.cpp`, so the front end needs it passed in beside `s_settings`. A preview says nothing; the voices' log lines are the drive scripts' evidence (`cue <voice> preview start`, `cue preview: <id>`).
- **For issue 05.** CLAUDE.md's cue engine status line could name `MELEE_A11Y_CUE_PREVIEW` beside the dump and trace when the feature's status line is written.
- **Left open.** The moments' lengths, pulse counts, the hum's ramp and the ledge's distance are first proposals for the play test (issue 06).
