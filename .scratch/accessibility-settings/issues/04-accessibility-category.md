# 04 The Accessibility category in the launcher and the port menu

Status: resolved (2026-10-20)
Type: task
Blocked by: 02, 03

`.scratch/accessibility-settings/spec.md`, "The Accessibility category", "Rows" and "Speech"; ADR-0007.

## Machinery

- **The wiring in the base port's files**, each line marked with an `a11y` comment and nothing more:
  - `tab-accessibility` in `launcher.cpp`'s `tab_ids`, before `tab-launcher`; the launcher's `page_focus` `case 5` becomes `case 6`
  - a category button and an empty page in `resources/launcher.rml` and `resources/port-menu.rml`
  - one line in each class's `page_focus` returning the fork's row ids
  - a one-line hand-off at the top of the handlers that act on an element id (spec open question 2), declared in `a11y_hooks.h`
- **The rows.** The fork builds them into the empty page from the model's shown list (issue 01) in the base port's markup classes, writes their values, saves through the model and plays the menu's own sounds as the base port's rows do. Switches step with Left, Right and Enter; sliders step with Left and Right by the value's step; Preview rows call issue 03's preview on Enter or A, and leaving the row or closing the menu stops it.
- **Speech.** The existing readers read the rows. Check that the switches' last announcements (issue 02) come out right from both pages, and that the status line rule still holds (spec open question 3).
- **Tests.** `test_launcher_speech.cpp`, `test_port_menu_speech.cpp` and `drive.py`'s `FOCUS_LINE` learn the new category.

## Done when

- In both pages, by keyboard and by controller (through the event pipe's `key` and `gamepad` steps), the category opens, every row is reached and changed, Preview plays, and `a11y.ini` holds the changes.
- The readers' headless tests cover the category's first row, a switch, a slider and a Preview row in both pages.
- `grep -rn a11y src CMakeLists.txt resources --exclude-dir=a11y` lists every changed base port line.
- The `verify` skill's menus level passes, and `python tools/check_style.py`.
- CLAUDE.md's isolation rule names the category's wiring as footprint, citing ADR-0007, and its "Fork settings" bullet describes the accessibility settings and `a11y.ini`.

## Comments

### 2026-10-20, implementation (agent)

Implemented; the Accessibility category sits in the launcher's Settings after Online and in the port menu last before Resume, its rows built from the accessibility settings.

- **Where things live.** `settings_page.hpp/.cpp`: an RmlUi plugin (`settings_pages_start`, registered in `pc_a11y_init` once every feature has registered its values) builds the rows into the empty `#page-accessibility` as `launcher.rml` or `port-menu.rml` loads, one `Page` per document, walking `AccessibilitySettings::shown()`. The same file defines the hand-off hooks, so the readers' tests call the real ones. Row ids are `a11y-` and the key with dots as dashes (`a11y-steering-on`, `a11y-cues-volume`, `a11y-gap_warning-preview`, `a11y-ledge_warning-distance`), a slider's shown value at the id and `-val`, as the base port's. `AccessibilitySettings::saves()` is new.
- **The markup**, in the base port's classes: a `row` with `data-desc` (the value's description), a `name`, and `button.value` for a switch ("On"/"Off"), the slider in `slider-control` with its `slider-value` span ("80%", "40 units"), or `button.value action` reading "Play" for Preview; in the port menu the help is repeated under the name in a `detail` span, as its own rows do. A value an environment override holds shows " (environment)" after it ("Off (environment)"), as the launcher shows an overridden vertical sync; a change from the menu lifts the override, and the suffix with it.
- **The wiring in `launcher.cpp`**, every line marked `a11y`: `tab-accessibility` in `tab_ids` (under `// clang-format off`, since clang-format reflowed the whole array and left unmarked lines), `case 5` returning `pc_a11y_settings_rows()` in both `page_focus`, the launcher's old `case 5` as `case 6`, and two-line hand-offs (`if (hook) return;`, which clang-format splits; both lines marked) at the top of both `is_slider`, `Launcher::action` and `PortMenu::apply` (`pc_a11y_settings_press`), `Launcher::horizontal` and `PortMenu::step` (`pc_a11y_settings_step`), and both Change branches (`pc_a11y_settings_change`, taking the event). The `.rml` files each gain the button and the page, an `<!-- a11y ... -->` comment on each line. The hooks are declared in a C++-only section at the end of `a11y_hooks.h`, after `extern "C"`, since they take `std::string`, `Rml::ElementDocument*` and `Rml::Event&`; C files see none of it. `CLAUDE.md`'s grep gains `resources`.
- **Spec open question 2: where the hand-offs sit.** `is_slider` knows the fork's sliders, so the pointer's hover, `typing()` (the launcher's Left and Right) and Enter treat them as sliders. Press (`action`/`apply`) covers Enter through RmlUi's click, A or Start through `activate_focus`, and the pointer. Step (`horizontal`/`step`) covers Left and Right by D-pad, stick and, on a switch, keys. The keyboard's arrows on a slider never reach the pages' listeners: RmlUi's own slider takes them in the target phase and fires Change, so the Change hand-off covers them and dragging. `cycle`, `step_slider`, `drag` and `activate_focus` need none. Who plays the sounds: the fork, in the port menu only (the launcher plays none), through a callback `hooks.cpp` binds to `lbAudioAx_80024030`, as the port menu plays its own: confirm for a switch, move for a slider's step or drag (also at the end of its range, as the base port's sliders do). Preview plays no menu sound, as the cue is the answer; Enter or A on a slider does nothing, rather than the base port's wrap from the top, a jump the player cannot hear coming. Left on Preview goes back to the category, as on the base port's action buttons.
- **Spec open question 3: "Saved."** After a change that was saved (a file in use), the port menu's status line shows "Saved.", as after its own rows; the reader never speaks it (`port_menu.drive`'s check and a headless test).
- **Spec open question 7: refresh.** No hand-off in the base port's refresh code: the fork writes its rows' values itself, at once after a change, and every frame from both frame hooks (`settings_pages_frame`), writing only what changed, so a hand edit or a lifted override shows too. The frame hooks run it before the reader reads the page, the port menu's before the speech gate. A slider's own write echoing back as a Change is ignored.
- **Preview.** Enter, A or a click calls `CueSystem::preview(group)`; the page keeps the row that started it and stops it (`CueSystem::stop_preview`) on the first frame its row lacks focus, the port menu is closed, or the page unloads (the launcher closing); it never stops a preview it did not start (`MELEE_A11Y_CUE_PREVIEW`'s).
- **Switches.** Their last and first announcements need nothing of the page: `set` reaches the listener, and the readers' `follow_focus` announces the row after "Speech on" or "Accessibility on". Checked headless in both pages and in the game (launcher: Accessibility off and on; port menu: Speech off and on).
- **Tests.** `test_rmlui_page.hpp` gains `A11ySettings` (the switches, the cues' shared values, the gap and ledge warnings and the ledge distance, no file, the game's side recorded) and the Change hand-off's listener; both reader tests start it, mirror the new `tab_ids` and `page_focus`, and cover the category's rows read in order (launcher) and its place before Resume (port menu), a switch by Left, Right and press, a slider by the hand-off and by the keyboard through RmlUi's Change, Enter on it doing nothing, a hand edit shown, Preview played, restarted, and stopped by leaving the row, by closing the launcher and by closing the port menu, the port menu's sounds, and Speech and Accessibility off and on from their rows. `drive.py`'s `FOCUS_LINE` learns the category by itself, from the `.rml` button. `launcher_settings.drive` and `port_menu.drive` step over the new category, and the `verify` skill says so.
