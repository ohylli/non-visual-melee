# 01 Submenus in the Accessibility category

Status: resolved (2026-10-30)
Type: task

`.scratch/accessibility-submenus/spec.md`, "The layout", "The rows that open a submenu", "Moving", "The title line" and "The wiring in the base port's files"; ADR-0009.

## Machinery

- **The layout.** The top level holds the five master switches, with Cue settings after Cues and Match announcement settings after Match announcements; Voice settings is the first row of Match announcement settings. A shown value says which submenu it belongs to as it is registered, so a new cue lands in Cue settings by itself (spec open question 1).
- **The rows that open a submenu,** with the spec's labels and descriptions, in markup a sighted helper sees as a row like the others (spec open question 5; the spoken form is issue 02's).
- **Moving.** Enter, A, a click or Right opens a submenu on its first row. Up and Down go round its rows in both pages: the launcher through `pc_a11y_settings_rows()` listing the open submenu's rows, the port menu through the fork moving focus in `pc_a11y_port_menu_step`, ahead of the speech gate (spec open questions 2 and 3). Escape, B, or Left on a row holding no value goes back a level, to the opening row.
- **The Back hooks.** One new hook at the top of `Launcher::back` and of `PortMenu::back`, marked with an `a11y` comment, declared in `a11y_hooks.h`'s C++ section.
- **The way back to the top level** when the port menu closes, the launcher's Settings is left, the page unloads, or the pointer takes focus out of the page (spec open question 7).
- **The title line** at the top of an open submenu.
- **Tests.** In both readers' headless tests, by focus: opening by each key, the loop at both ends, back by Escape, B and Left on a Preview, Left and Right still stepping a value, the nested Voice settings two levels in and out, closing or leaving from inside a submenu and reopening at the top level, and moving with Speech off. `A11ySettings` in `test_rmlui_page.hpp` gains a nested submenu, and the copies of both pages' `back` the new hook.

## Done when

- In both pages, by keyboard and by controller (through the event pipe's `key` and `gamepad` steps), every row of every submenu is reached and changed, and every way back lands where the spec says.
- `grep -rn a11y src CMakeLists.txt resources --exclude-dir=a11y` lists the two new hook lines and nothing else new.
- The headless tests above pass, as does `python tools/check_style.py`.
- The drive scripts that step into the category may fail until issue 03; note which.

## Comments

### 2026-10-30, implementation (agent)

Implemented; the Accessibility category's top level holds the five master switches with Cue settings after Cues and Match announcement settings after Match announcements, Voice settings first inside the latter, in both pages.

- **Spec open question 1: a value names its submenu through its group handle.** `value_model.hpp` gains `NamedMenu` (key, label, description, parent) and `ValueGroup::add_menu`, which registers the opening row where it is called; `ValueGroup::in(menu)` gives a handle whose shown rows go into that submenu, and a handle handed on keeps it. `ShownRow` gains `opens` (the submenu a row opens) and `menu` (the one it is in). Each level's rows are in registration order. So `CueSettings` registers Cues, then Cue settings, then everything else through `in(&m_menu)`, and `add_cue` and `add_shared` hand out groups already in Cue settings: a new cue, or a value a cue shows (relative to you, follow the camera, ledge distance), lands there with no other change. `MatchAnnouncementSettings` registers its switch, Match announcement settings, then Voice settings as that submenu's first row, and the rest inside; `VoiceSettings` takes the submenu its rows go into (`MatchAnnouncementSettings::voice_menu()` in the game). The top level's order came from reordering registration in `pc_a11y_init`: the switches, the cue system, the match announcements, then the voices; `Switches`' `after_speech` went, unused. A fresh `a11y.ini` now has the voices' and the match announcements' groups after the cues; an existing file keeps its order, as the line edit keeps it. Submenus are never in the file.
- **Spec open question 2: `pc_a11y_settings_rows()` answers for the page with a submenu open, else the top level.** Checked: `pc_launcher_run` closes the launcher's document (and updates the context, unloading it) before `pc_menu_init` loads the port menu's, and each page shows its top level again once focus leaves its rows, so at most one page has a submenu open. The open submenu is still kept per page (`Page::m_open`).
- **Spec open question 3: the port menu's step.** `pc_a11y_port_menu_step` calls `settings_pages_step(port_menu, direction)` first, ahead of the speech gate and the reader's line step: inside a submenu it moves focus round the submenu's rows and returns true. The reader says the row focus lands on from its next frame, as for any focus move (headless tests, and the drive runs below). Being ahead of the line step loses nothing: the category has no lines between its rows.
- **Spec open question 5, the markup.** A row opening a submenu is a `div.row` with its name (and in the port menu its `detail`), and a `button` of the classes `value action submenu` labelled "Open", its id from its key (`a11y-cues-settings`, `a11y-match_announcements-settings`, `a11y-match_announcements-voices`). A sighted helper sees it as the Preview rows' Play buttons are seen. Until issue 02 the reader reads it as a button in a row: "Cue settings: Open, button. <description>".
- **Spec open question 7, and every other way out: the open level follows focus.** Every level's rows are built at once and the other levels' rows hidden (`display: none` on the row). Each page listens to Focus on its document: a row taking focus shows its own level, focus anywhere else (a category, Back, Resume) shows the top level. Its Hide event does the same, so closing the port menu by any way (F1, Start, the menu button, Resume, Quit game) brings the top level back before `show_tab` copies its column at the next opening; `settings_pages_frame(host, false)` does it again as a backstop, and an unloaded page simply goes. A click inside the open submenu focuses one of its rows and stays there.
- **Moving.** Enter, A or a click (`pc_a11y_settings_press`) and Right (`pc_a11y_settings_step`) on an opening row open its submenu on the first row. Left on a row holding no value (a Preview, an opening row) goes back a level inside a submenu; on the top level the step still returns false, so the page goes back to the category as before. The launcher walks the open level by itself, since `Launcher::column` asks `page_focus()` at every press; in the port menu, inside a submenu, Down never reaches Resume and Up never the category.
- **The Back hooks.** `pc_a11y_settings_back(document)` at the top of `Launcher::back` and `PortMenu::back`, an `if` and its `return` each, marked `a11y`, declared in `a11y_hooks.h`'s C++ section; true when a submenu was open and focus went to its opening row. The grep lists these and nothing else new.
- **The title line** is `#a11y-menu-title`, the page's first child, an `.info` block with the page's own heading (`h3` in the launcher, `h2` in the port menu): "Cue settings", "Match announcement settings / Voice settings", hidden on the top level. Opening a submenu scrolls it into view above the first row.
- **Spec open question 6, taken here.** Without it the launcher's line reading (which wraps) stopped on the title line after the first row and the last, an extra press in the loop. `page_stops` now skips a row marked `aria-hidden="true"`, which the title line is. Issue 02 keeps the test of the words.
- **Tests.** `test_rmlui_page.hpp`'s `A11ySettings` registers the real `MatchAnnouncementSettings` and, in its Voice settings, the tests' own choice and a Preview, so the harness has the real top level and the nested submenu; shared lists of each level's ids, and helpers checking which level is shown and the title line. The copies of both pages' `back` gain the hook; the port menu's copy of `focus_step` now walks `focus_ids` copied in `show_tab`, as `PortMenu` does, and calls `settings_pages_step` before the reader's step. New cases in both suites: the top level read in its order (in the port menu on to Resume); opening by Enter and Right, with the title and only the submenu's rows shown; the loop at both ends; values still stepping; Left on a Preview and Escape back to the opening row, then Escape on to the category; Right on a Preview and Left on a top-level opening row left to the page; Voice settings two levels in and out by Left and by Escape; leaving the launcher's Settings, Back taken by the pointer and a category from inside a submenu; closing the port menu by toggle and by Quit game, a shoulder button and a click on a category, each reopening at the top level with Down from its last row on to Resume; a click inside the submenu staying; moving with Speech off. `accessibility_settings` gains a nested submenu case; `match_cues`, `switches`, `match_announcer` and `voice_settings` check where their rows now sit.
