# 03 Stocks and the Teammates switch

Status: resolved (2026-10-28)
Type: task
Blocked by: 01

Spec: `.scratch/match-announcements/spec.md`, "Stocks" and "Who is announced, in which voice".

## What to build

- **Stocks.** In stock matches only, said on the tick the count changes, rises and falls alike: "2 stocks", "last stock" at one, after the label where there is one ("P2, 2 stocks", "CPU 3, last stock"). At 0 nothing: the game's announcer calls the elimination. A team stock steal raises a count and is said the same way (open question 4).
- **The rows**, after the damage rows: "Your stocks" and "Opponents' stocks", switches, on by default; "Teammates", a switch, on by default, whether teammates are announced at all, damage and stocks.
- **A stock line replaces** a waiting damage line about the same player, as the voices' queueing rules give for one subject.

## Done when

- The announcer's unit test covers a loss, last stock, 0, a steal, each switch, a teammate off and on, a timed match with no stock lines, and a stock line replacing a waiting damage line.
- A drive run of the debug match with stocks shows the stock lines of both players.
- `cmake --build build` passes, as do the unit tests and `python tools/check_style.py`.
- `docs/a11y/gameplay-state.md` has the answer to open question 4.

## Comments

### 2026-10-28, implementation (agent)

Implemented; each player's stocks are said as they change, in the voice that player's damage uses, and teammates can be left out.

- **Where things live.** `game_access.c`: `A11yMatchHud.stocks`, whether the HUD shows stocks. `match_announcer.hpp/.cpp`: `stocks()`, run each tick for every followed player before the damage, `stock_line`, a `teammate` flag and the stocks last said in `Player`, and `MatchAnnouncementValues::your_stocks`, `opponents_stocks` and `teammates`; `damage_mode()` and `stocks_on()` give Off for a teammate while the Teammates switch is off, tested on the slots' teams (`teammate()`, which `label()` now shares). `match_announcement_settings.hpp/.cpp`: the switches `match_announcements.your_stocks`, `opponents_stocks` and `teammates`, registered after the interval and before the settle time. No hook or `hooks.cpp` change.
- **The rule as built.** A count is said on the tick it differs from the last said, rises and falls alike: "2 stocks", "last stock" at one, after the label with a comma ("CPU 3, last stock", "teammate CPU 2, 2 stocks"). At 0 nothing, the count still taken as said, so a steal from 0 says "last stock". With no stocks on the HUD, the Match announcements switch off, the side's switch off, or a teammate behind Teammates, a change is taken as said, so turning a switch on says only later changes. A change while the HUD is hidden waits until it shows, as damage does. At GO and when a panel appears, the count shown is the baseline. The Teammates switch leaves the labels alone: they still count teammates at GO, so the wording never depends on a switch.
- **Which matches have stocks: the HUD's test, wider than "stock matches".** `stocks` is `gm_8016B094() || gm_8016B0E8()`: a stock match, or a mode counting lives (`rules.x4_2`: Classic, Adventure, Multi-Man), the test by which `ifStatus_802F6508` gives each panel its stock icons and `ftCo_800D34E0` takes a stock. So Classic's lives are said too, as the screen shows them. In 10-Man Melee six wireframes knocked out gave no stock line: each leaves its slot at its KO (1 to 0, said as nothing) and the next one in the slot starts afresh.
- **Open question 4: the taker's panel is there on the steal's tick.** `fn_8016B918` runs only in VS with teams; in VS no panel is taken down at an elimination (`rules.x2_7` is set only by single-player modes, `gm_17C0.c`), and the respawn it calls, `fn_8016719C`, runs `ifStatus_802F6508`, making or reusing the panel on that tick, before the tick hook. The eliminated fighter stays on the fighter list, so the slot stays present: in `match_stock_steal.drive` both counts were said as the Start press reached the game. Recorded in `docs/a11y/gameplay-state.md`, "A team's stock steal".
- **A finding for the play test: a damage line can take a stock line's place.** The steal's giver is usually mid-fight, so its next damage figure settles a moment later and, by the one-subject rule, replaces the stock line still waiting or cuts it off while it plays: in two of the four runs of the steal script `"teammate CPU 2, 2 stocks"` was dropped (`dropped (replaced)`) or cut 300 ms in by `"teammate CPU 2 30"`. After a KO this cannot happen, since the knocked-out player owes no damage until the respawn and the next hit. Left as the spec's rule gives it; if the play test minds, a damage line could wait while a stock line about the same player waits or plays, which needs the voices to tell the announcer.
- **A finding outside this issue (issue 01's damage): Multi-Man's wireframes say "0".** In 10-Man Melee a wireframe knocked out was followed by `"CPU 5 0"` and the like: the next wireframe in the slot shows 0% after the last one's figure, with no KO state seen between. Whether wireframes are announced at all is already the maintainer's call (issue 01); if they stay, that reset wants a look.
